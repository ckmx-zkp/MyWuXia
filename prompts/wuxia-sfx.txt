# 江湖长夜 · 音效生成提示词

> 用途：喂给 ElevenLabs Sound Effects / Stable Audio / Suno SFX 模式。所有提示词均含中文用途、英文 prompt、长度与采样参数。
> 通用约束：`mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit WAV; normalized -12 LUFS; max 4 sec unless ambient; seamless loop markers if > 6 sec.`

---

## 生成参数（SFX）

```text
model: elevenlabs-sound-effects / stable-audio
sample rate: 44.1 kHz
bit depth: 16-bit
channels: 1 (mono) for SFX, 2 for ambient loops
format: WAV
normalization: -12 LUFS peak -3 dBFS
max duration: 4 sec (SFX) / 60 sec (ambient)
loop markers: at 0 and end, seamless crossfade 80 ms
```
